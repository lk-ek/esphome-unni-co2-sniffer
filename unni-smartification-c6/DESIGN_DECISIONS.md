# Design decisions

- Original Unni board signal traces remain untouched; sensor and touch/backlight connections are T-taps.
- The original small USB board is replaced, allowing USB_5V_RAW to be separated from muxed UNNI_AC.
- External USB is TPS2116 VIN1 and therefore has priority over internally boosted 5 V.
- Battery remains directly connected to UNNI_DC.
- ESP 3.3 V is generated by TPS63031 buck-boost from the BQ24074 system output.
- ESP32-C6 native USB is routed through the replacement USB-C connector.
- GPIO4/5/8/9/15 are C6 strapping pins; runtime Unni signals avoid them. GPIO9 is reserved for BOOT only.
